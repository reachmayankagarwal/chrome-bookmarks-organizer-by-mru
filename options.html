Privacy

  • Stored locally (this device only, never synced): click counts, safety snapshots, duplicate map, device ID and name.
  • Synced via Chrome Sync (shared across all your Chrome profiles): schedule settings, MRU settings, sort settings, known devices list, and primary device ID.
  • What webNavigation sees: only navigations classified as auto_bookmark — i.e. navigations triggered by clicking a bookmark. It does not see page contents, form data, browsing history, or any navigation that was not from a bookmark click.
  • Click data is per-device: counts and history remain on the device where you clicked. They are never uploaded to any server or shared with any third party.
